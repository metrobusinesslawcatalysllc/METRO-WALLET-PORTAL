Demo settlement rules

At expiry, a higher generated quote settles CALL as a win; a lower quote settles PUT as a win. A correct prediction returns the stake plus an 80% demo payout. An incorrect prediction loses the virtual stake. An unchanged quote returns the virtual stake. These simplified rules are for interface testing only.

All balances, quotes, trade records, wins, and losses are simulated in this browser only. Clearing browser storage resets this demo. Not an offer to trade or a real-money brokerage service.
